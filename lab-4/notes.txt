## Column Legend
 
- **GP** – Games Played
- **W** – Wins
- **L** – Losses
- **OTL** – Overtime Losses
- **PTS** – Points
- **RW** – Regulation Wins
- **ROW** – Regulation + Overtime Wins
- **GF** – Goals For
- **GA** – Goals Against
- **DIFF** – Goal Differential
- **STRK** – Current Streak
- **L10** – Record in Last 10 Games
